## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an esoteric yet approachable digital tabletop interface for an MBTI-themed card and archetype game. It unites the mysterious intimacy of Victorian occult tarot decks with the high-legibility convenience of modern tactical digital games. 

The visual style strikes a calculated balance between historic bookbinding craftsmanship and contemporary minimalism. Heavy, ink-bathed obsidian backdrops and warm antique parchment substrates are framed by razor-thin, metallic gilded filigree. Functional UI chrome—such as player statistics, turn logs, inventory grids, and interactive game mechanics—is stripped of historical clutter, presenting razor-sharp typographic clarity and disciplined spatial hierarchy. 

Personality groups are immediately differentiated through disciplined, muted jewel and pastel accents, grounding ancient mysticism into structured psychological gameplay. The overall emotional tone is cerebral, collectible, prestigious, and enchanting.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an opulent obsidian-and-gilded foundation, elevated by tactile parchment tones and distinct group identification tokens:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`)**: Antique leaf gold. Deployed for primary actions, triumph states, selected card outlines, filigree ornamentation, and prominent numeric indicators.
- **Secondary (`#1C1B1A`)**: Polished charcoal obsidian. Supplies surface container tiers, card frames, modal drawers, and elevated interactive regions.
- **Tertiary (`#E8DEC8`)**: Aged vellum parchment. Utilized for warm high-contrast text headlines, interior card illustration backplates, and subtle ambient highlights.
- **Neutral (`#0D0C0B`)**: Deep void black. Serves as the canvas substrate, grounding deep game board surfaces and underlay sheets.

### MBTI Role Archetype Tones
Four disciplined accent hues delineate personality taxonomy across badges, energy pips, and card aura highlights:
- **Analysts (`#A78BFA`)**: Ethereal amethyst purple.
- **Diplomats (`#4ADE80`)**: Sage emerald green.
- **Sentinels (`#60A5FA`)**: Celestial sapphire blue.
- **Explorers (`#FBBF24`)**: Warm solar amber.

Functional states adhere to this antique spectrum: errors present as crimson sealing wax (`#C2410C`), success manifests in bright gilded radiance (`#FACC15`), and disabled states settle into faded graphite (`#3F3D39`).

## Typography

The typographical pairing reinforces the contrast between narrative antiquarian lore and practical tactical decision-making:

- **Display & Headlines (`Libre Caslon Text`)**: Imparts an authoritative literary provenance. Card banners, phase transitions, victory declarations, and modal headers leverage this historic serif. Card titles (`title-card`) intentionally use uppercase letterforms with extended letter spacing to echo vintage deck printing traditions.
- **Body, Badges, and Stats (`Manrope`)**: A clean, balanced geometric sans-serif that ensures rapid legibility across combat logs, ability descriptions, resource counters, and settings panels.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Narrative passages and card archetype titling rely strictly on `Libre Caslon Text`. Any numeric, statistical, or cooldown metadata is delivered via `Manrope` at semibold and bold weights to avoid visual fatigue during fast-paced play.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances the containment requirements of collectible card grids with atmospheric peripheral negative space:

- **Grid Architecture**: Hand areas and deck matrices run on a fluid 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet screens, shifting to a 4-column adaptive layout on mobile viewports. On active combat stages, elements use a card-ratio preservation grid (standard 5:7 tarot aspect ratio) with fixed gutters to prevent card art distortion.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Mobile (< 640px)**: Margins compress to `1rem` (`space-md`). Playing hands shift to a layered horizontal carousel or fan stack. Modals and detail inspect sheets slide up as bottom sheets.
  - **Tablet (640px – 1024px)**: Outer margins expand to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), revealing dual-lane battlefield boards and docked side rails.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: Canvas margins set at `2.5rem` (`space-xl`), flanked by dedicated deck graveyards, status monitors, and contextual inspection panels.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Micro component layouts (button padding, trait badges, stat slots) strictly leverage modular multiples of `0.25rem` (`4px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic drop shadows, prioritizing tactile card thickness, metallic gleam, and back-lit atmospheric halos:

- **Ground Plane (Board Surface)**: Base color `#0D0C0B` overlaid with a subtle noise or cross-hatched antique linen texture.
- **Card Tier 1 (In Hand / Resting)**: Surface `#1C1B1A` bounded by a 1px solid border of `#D4AF37` at 25% opacity. Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Card Tier 2 (Hover / Ready State)**: Lifted via translation (`translateY(-8px)`). Border brightness increases to 70% primary gold. Shadow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- **Active / Dragging State**: Scaled to 1.05x with an ambient occult rim-glow matching the card’s MBTI role accent color (`rgba(role_color, 0.35)`), accompanied by a deep shadow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9)`).
- **HUD & Modal Drawers**: Backed with 92% opaque `#1C1B1A` and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, edged with a razor-thin 1px gold hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor crisp, near-chiseled corners with a gentle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, avoiding overly circular geometry to maintain card-stock authenticity:

- **Cards**: Outer frame corners use `rounded-sm` (4px) or `rounded-md` (6px) to emulate high-grade cut card stock. Internal filigree frames have notched, mitered, or inset right angles.
- **Controls & Buttons**: Standardized at `0.25rem` border radius. Ghost variants and action plates honor structured rectilinear silhouettes.
- **Badges & Archetype Chips**: Use subtle `0.25rem` soft corners. Pill shapes are forbidden; tags must feel like wax-stamped ribbons, metallic ingots, or scholarly book plates.

## Components

### 1. Archetype Cards (Tarot TCG Core)
- **Structure**: Dual-sided layout in 5:7 aspect ratio. 
  - **Light Vellum Variant**: Background `#E8DEC8`, dark ink type `#0D0C0B`, framed by rich gold filigree borders (`#8A7120`).
  - **Obsidian Arcana Variant**: Background `#1C1B1A`, bone parchment text `#E8DEC8`, framed by luminous metallic gold filigree (`#D4AF37`).
- **Elements**: 
  - Header: Centered MBTI archetype tag (e.g., "ARCHITECT", "MEDIATOR") flanked by Roman numeral tier markers.
  - Art Window: Inner decorative box ringed with floral and geometric corner spandrels.
  - Footer: Bold 4-letter type identifier (e.g., "INTJ", "ENFP") in `Libre Caslon Text`, flanked by role icon badges.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary (Gilded Relic)**: Background `#D4AF37`, text `#0D0C0B`, semibold `Manrope`. Subtly beveled 1px inner highlight, hover transition into a glowing amber tint with an expanded soft gold outline.
- **Secondary (Obsidian Plate)**: Background `#1C1B1A`, 1px border `#D4AF37` at 40% opacity, text `#E8DEC8`. Hover brings 100% border luminescence.
- **Ghost / Utility**: Borderless, text `#E8DEC8` with gold underline on focus/hover.

### 3. Chips & Archetype Badges
- Compact rectangular markers (`0.25rem` radius). Dark obsidian interior paired with a 1px border tinted in the designated archetype color (Purple, Green, Blue, or Amber). Text uses `label-archetype` in full uppercase with tracking.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#131211` with a recessed inner inset shadow. Bottom border or full 1px border in subdued brass (`#54451D`). Focused state ignites a full `#D4AF37` hairline stroke without soft outer rings. Placeholder text set in muted parchment grey.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Toggles
- Custom geometric icons. Checkboxes: Rectangular brass boxes with an etched antique checkmark or runic slash. Radios: Inset diamond lozenges that fill with a radiant gold star glyph when active.

### 6. Inspect & Lore Modals
- Central floating or side-anchored panels. Double-stroke borders (outer 1px solid brass, inner 1px dashed hairline parchment) recreating traditional illuminated manuscripts.
